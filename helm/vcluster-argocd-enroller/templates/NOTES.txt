To check the operator status:

To view operator logs:
  kubectl logs -l app.kubernetes.io/name={{ include "vcluster-argocd-enroller.name" . }},app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }} -n {{ include "vcluster-argocd-enroller.vclusterNamespace" . }}

The operator will:
1. Watch for vClusters (StatefulSets and Deployments labelled app=vcluster) in all namespaces
2. Extract the credentials from each vCluster's vc-<name> secret
3. Enroll it in ArgoCD as cluster <namespace>-<name> (secret vcluster-<namespace>-<name>)
4. Keep that secret in sync, and remove it when the vCluster is deleted

For more information, visit: https://github.com/andrewrothstein/vcluster-argocd-enroller
